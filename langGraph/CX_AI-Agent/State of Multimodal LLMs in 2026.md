# State of Multimodal LLMs in 2026

## Introduction to Multimodal LLMs
Multimodal LLMs (Large Language Models) are AI models that can process and generate multiple forms of data, such as text, images, and audio [([What is a Multimodal LLM](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/multimodal-llm))](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/multimodal-llm). They have the capability to understand and generate human-like language, as well as perceive and interpret visual and auditory information. 
* Recent advancements in Multimodal LLMs have led to significant improvements in their performance and capabilities, with applications in materials science [([A multimodal large language model for materials science](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01214-y))](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01214-y) and other fields.
* The importance of Multimodal LLMs in AI development lies in their ability to enable machines to interact with humans in a more natural and intuitive way, using multiple forms of communication [([The State of Multimodal AI: What VLMs Can Actually Do](https://www.codesota.com/guides/multimodal-ai))](https://www.codesota.com/guides/multimodal-ai).

## Current Applications of Multimodal LLMs
Multimodal LLMs have been applied in various fields, showcasing their versatility and potential. 
* Text-to-image generation and vision-language understanding are two significant applications of Multimodal LLMs. According to [What is a Multimodal LLM (MLLM)?](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/multimodal-llm), these models can generate images from text prompts and understand the relationship between images and text. 
* In materials science, Multimodal LLMs have been used to analyze and understand the properties of materials. A study published in [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01214-y) demonstrates the use of a multimodal large language model for materials science, highlighting its potential in this field.

As discussed in [The State of Multimodal AI: What VLMs Can Actually Do](https://www.codesota.com/guides/multimodal-ai), these agents can perform tasks that require multiple forms of input and output.

## Challenges and Limitations of Multimodal LLMs
State-of-the-art Multimodal LLMs struggle with spatial tasks, such as understanding the relationship between objects in an image or generating text based on visual input [([The State of Multimodal AI: What VLMs Can Actually Do](https://www.codesota.com/guides/multimodal-ai))](https://www.codesota.com/guides/multimodal-ai). This limitation is due to the complexity of processing and integrating multiple modalities, including images, text, and audio. 
* Multimodal LLMs have limitations in processing these modalities, including:
  + Inability to fully understand the context and nuances of visual and auditory data
  + Difficulty in generating coherent and relevant text based on multimodal input
  + Struggling to capture the subtle relationships between different modalities
As noted in [([Multimodal AI in 2026: Vision + Text + Audio](https://jamesm.blog/ai/multimodal-ai-2026))](https://jamesm.blog/ai/multimodal-ai-2026), these limitations highlight the need for further research and development in Multimodal LLMs. 
To overcome these challenges, researchers and developers must continue to advance the field of Multimodal LLMs, exploring new architectures, training methods, and applications [([Beyond Text: The Rise of Large Multimodal Models -A 2026 Deep Dive](https://medium.com/@adityaj5400/beyond-text-the-rise-of-large-multimodal-models-a-2026-deep-dive-0843292fa048))](https://medium.com/@adityaj5400/beyond-text-the-rise-of-large-multimodal-models-a-2026-deep-dive-0843292fa048).
